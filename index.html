2. What the bot does

a. is a video editing utility for Discord, built on FFmpeg. It lets you trim, convert, compress, resize, merge, and otherwise process video and audio files you upload or link to, directly from Discord commands. It's provided as-is, free of charge unless stated otherwise, and we can add, remove, or change features (including supported formats and size limits) at any time without notice.

5. Data we collect

To process a request, the bot temporarily downloads the video or audio file you provide, runs it through FFmpeg, and uploads the result back to Discord. Files are deleted from our servers automatically once processing finishes or fails — we don't keep a media library of what users upload. We do store lightweight metadata needed to operate: server IDs, user IDs, command usage, and configuration settings you've set up. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown, including exact retention windows and how to request deletion.
